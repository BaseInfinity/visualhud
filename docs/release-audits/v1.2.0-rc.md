## Asset Provenance Completion Audit

- The retained tarball contains 55 packaged branded PNGs; specifically,
  53 theme sprites plus two contact sheets.
- All 18 packaged Pokemon sprites byte-for-byte match PokeAPI HOME files. Each
  source file is the corresponding 512x512 numbered render.
  The repository still needs a package-visible manifest binding those exact
  hashes to their upstream sources and notices.
- PokeAPI invites application use and labels its sprite repository CC0, while
  its `LICENCE.txt` also says the image contents are copyrighted by The
  Pokémon Company, limits the waiver to rights held by the affirmer, preserves
  trademark rights, and disclaims third-party clearance. That evidence proves
  provenance but does not by itself prove that the images are public domain.
- The TMNT manifest documents crop provenance. One April source is labeled
  CC-BY-SA, but most entries do not record an asset-specific license,
  attribution requirement, or permission. The exact requirements for every
  retained TMNT file therefore remain incomplete.
- [Pokémon Support](https://support.pokemon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000634094-Can-I-use-Pok%C3%A9mon-images-or-materials),
  [Pokémon copyright guidance](https://www.pokemon.com/us/legal/copyright), and
  [Paramount's terms](https://www.paramount.com/terms-of-use) do not provide the
  permission needed to redistribute these branded files through npm.
